How It Works

Step 01

Upload Your Clothes

Take photos of the clothes you want to transform. Our AI system will analyze the fabric, style, and potential for upcycling.

  • Multiple angle photos for better analysis
  • Fabric type identification
  • Style potential assessment
Step 02

Explore Design Ideas

Receive AI-generated design suggestions based on current trends and your style preferences.

  • Personalized style recommendations
  • Trend-based suggestions
  • Sustainable transformation options
Step 03

Connect with Tailors

Browse profiles of skilled local tailors and choose the perfect match for your project.

  • View tailor portfolios and ratings
  • Compare pricing and timelines
  • Direct communication channel
