Our Story

Revolutionizing the way people connect with tailors and transform their clothing

Our Mission

At UpThreaded, we're on a mission to revolutionize the tailoring industry by connecting talented tailors with customers seeking quality clothing alterations and custom designs.

We believe in sustainable fashion and giving new life to existing garments through expert tailoring. Our platform makes it easy for anyone to find skilled tailors and transform their wardrobe.

Our Vision & Values

Sustainability First

We're committed to reducing fashion waste by promoting upcycling and sustainable practices. Every garment transformed through our platform helps reduce the environmental impact of the fashion industry.

Innovation Driven

Our AI-powered platform combines cutting-edge technology with traditional craftsmanship to create unique, personalized fashion solutions for every user.

Community Focused

We're building a community of conscious consumers and skilled artisans who share our passion for sustainable fashion and quality craftsmanship.

Quality Excellence

Every transformation through our platform meets the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship, ensuring customer satisfaction and lasting value.
